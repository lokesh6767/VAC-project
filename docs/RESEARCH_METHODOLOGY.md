# AI Insight — Research & Evidence Methodology

## 1. Principles of Verification & Truthful Attribution
AI Insight enforces strict empirical principles:
1. **Primary Source Primacy:** All product claims regarding capabilities, supported models, pricing tiers, token limits, and data privacy MUST originate from official vendor documentation or verified press releases from OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic.
2. **Dated Timestamps:** Generative AI features and pricing evolve rapidly. Every claim and citation contains both a `publicationDate` (when announced) and a `lastCheckedDate` (when verified by the research team).
3. **No Fabricated Data:** If a metric, pricing tier, or model capability cannot be verified in public documentation, it is marked as `unverified` or `unknown`. It is NEVER simulated or inferred without disclosure.
4. **Distinction Between Product Tiers:** Free consumer web apps (e.g. chatgpt.com free tier) are clearly distinguished from paid tiers (Plus, Pro), team workspaces, and developer APIs (pay-per-token).

## 2. Evidence Verification Taxonomy
- **`verified` (Green):** Supported by active, accessible official vendor documentation or technical whitepaper checked within the last 90 days.
- **`needs_review` (Amber):** The vendor announced recent changes, or the last verified date exceeds 90 days, requiring human re-audit.
- **`outdated` (Red):** The claim was superseded by a newer model or pricing change (e.g., GPT-3.5 deprecated in favor of GPT-4o mini; Gemini 1.0 replaced by 2.0).
- **`disputed` (Red/Amber):** Independent testing or community benchmarks show material discrepancy with marketing claims.
- **`unverified` (Slate/Gray):** Vendor has not published definitive documentation or technical specifications (e.g., exact parameter counts, training dataset composition).
